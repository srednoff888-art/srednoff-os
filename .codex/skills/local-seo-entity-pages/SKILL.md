# Local SEO Entity Pages

## Decisions

- Verify business entity, service areas, contact data, opening hours, and unique local content.
- Avoid doorway-page duplication and unsupported local claims; reconcile structured data with visible content.

## Acceptance

Check representative entity pages, canonical/indexability, and source facts.

## Constraints

- Do not generate doorway pages or fake locations.
