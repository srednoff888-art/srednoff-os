# Tokenomics Whitepaper Review

## Decisions

- Identify supply, emissions, unlocks, rights, liquidity, governance, and source claims.
- Reconcile paper assumptions with contracts and actual distribution; flag unknown privileged controls.

## Acceptance

Report evidence-backed economic risks and uncertainty without predicting price.

## Constraints

- Do not endorse token claims without source-backed analysis.
