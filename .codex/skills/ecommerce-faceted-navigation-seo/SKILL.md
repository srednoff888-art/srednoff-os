# Ecommerce Faceted Navigation SEO

## Task Decisions

1. Classify facet combinations by demand and inventory value, then choose crawl/index behavior consistently.
2. Test generated URLs, robots/canonical interactions and internal-link expansion to prevent combinatorial crawl traps.

Use this skill for SEO tasks focused on facets, filters, pagination, canonical/noindex strategy, crawl traps, product listing SEO.

## Guardrails

- Do not index infinite filter combinations without unique demand.
- Do not perform destructive, paid, production, publishing, account-changing, or externally visible actions without explicit user confirmation.
- Do not expose secrets, private keys, tokens, cookies, private analytics, personal data, or confidential business data.
- If validation is impossible, state why and provide a concrete manual verification path.
