# Google Ads Account Audit

## Decisions

- Confirm account scope, currency, date windows, conversion definitions, and reporting lag.
- Reconcile spend, search terms, campaign type, tracking quality, and landing-page fit before recommendations.

## Acceptance

Produce evidence-linked findings with priorities and draft changes; distinguish missing reports from zeros.

## Constraints

- Do not change budgets, bids, or live campaigns without explicit confirmation.
