# Attribution Incrementality Review

## Decisions

- Define the causal question, attribution windows, conversion unit, and overlap between channels.
- Prefer a valid holdout or geo experiment; check leakage, power, and pre-period balance.

## Acceptance

Report incremental estimates with uncertainty and distinguish them from platform ROAS.

## Constraints

- Do not claim deterministic attribution when data is modeled or incomplete.
