# Multilingual Content Localization SEO

## Task Decisions

1. Use a factual locale URL map with translated intent, local terminology and coherent canonical/hreflang clusters.
2. Check market-specific content and index eligibility; machine translation alone is not localized search relevance.

Use this skill for SEO tasks focused on translation SEO, locale keyword maps, regional intent, localized metadata and slugs.

## Guardrails

- Do not machine-translate sensitive or brand-critical pages without review.
- Do not perform destructive, paid, production, publishing, account-changing, or externally visible actions without explicit user confirmation.
- Do not expose secrets, private keys, tokens, cookies, private analytics, personal data, or confidential business data.
- If validation is impossible, state why and provide a concrete manual verification path.
