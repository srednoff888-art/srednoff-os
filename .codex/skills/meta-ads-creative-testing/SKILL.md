# Meta Ads Creative Testing

## Decisions

- Define creative hypothesis, audience, attribution window, conversion quality, and decision threshold.
- Separate fatigue from delivery changes and confounding budget/audience edits.

## Acceptance

Report controlled comparisons, uncertainty, measurement window, and a scoped next experiment.

## Constraints

- Do not use misleading claims or policy-sensitive creative.
