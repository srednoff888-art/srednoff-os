# SEO A/B Testing Causal Review

## Task Decisions

1. Define comparable page cohorts, assignment, interference risks and pre-period trends before a change.
2. Account for crawl lag and seasonality; confidence intervals or inconclusive outcomes are preferable to invented uplift.

Use this skill for SEO tasks focused on SEO experiments, causal analysis, rollouts, guardrails, interpretation of noisy organic data.

## Guardrails

- Do not overstate causality from uncontrolled SEO changes.
- Do not perform destructive, paid, production, publishing, account-changing, or externally visible actions without explicit user confirmation.
- Do not expose secrets, private keys, tokens, cookies, private analytics, personal data, or confidential business data.
- If validation is impossible, state why and provide a concrete manual verification path.
