# Portfolio Risk Rebalancing

## Decisions

- Establish holdings, liquidity, valuation timestamp, concentration limits, fees, and risk horizon.
- Model exposure and drawdown scenarios; separate proposed trades from authorized execution.

## Acceptance

Report assumptions, transaction cost, target allocations, and uncertainty without promising returns.

## Constraints

- Do not execute trades or custody changes without explicit approval.
