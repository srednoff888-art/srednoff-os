# Advertising Creative Rights Inventory

## Decisions And Evidence

1. Track each image, font, voice, likeness, music and template to source/license terms and the intended market/placement.
2. Deliver an asset-to-creative manifest with unresolved permissions, expiry and required notices; block publication of unresolved assets.

## Boundary

Public availability and AI generation do not establish endorsement, trademark or redistribution rights; do not purchase licenses automatically.

## Source

[Current primary reference](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6018015). Check the applicable asset license and platform requirements before publication; this workflow does not provide legal clearance.
