# Landing Page Conversion Design

## Decisions

- Map traffic intent, offer, proof, objections, and primary conversion to the page sequence.
- Keep real form/tracking behavior, mobile readability, and message match in the design contract.

## Acceptance

Exercise conversion and failure paths; use an experiment to claim uplift rather than visual preference.

## Constraints

- Do not make marketing pages that obscure the actual product or offer.
