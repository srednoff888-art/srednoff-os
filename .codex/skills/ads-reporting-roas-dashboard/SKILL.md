# Ads Reporting Roas Dashboard

## Decisions

- Define spend, revenue, currency, timezone, attribution window, refund handling, and deduplication before calculating ROAS.
- Show delayed or missing platform data explicitly; distinguish attributed revenue from incremental revenue.

## Acceptance

Reconcile dashboard totals with raw platform exports and test zero-spend and incomplete-period cases.

## Constraints

- Do not hide attribution uncertainty or reporting caveats.
