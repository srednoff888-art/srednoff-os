# Content Design Microcopy

## Decisions

- Write labels and recovery messages for real workflow states, keeping action, object, and consequence clear.
- Preserve legal/financial meaning and fit localization, screen-reader names, and available UI space.

## Acceptance

Review strings in rendered empty/error/success states rather than a text-only list.

## Constraints

- Do not use explanatory filler inside the app when clear UI affordances are better.
