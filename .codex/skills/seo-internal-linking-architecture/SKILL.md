# seo internal linking architecture

## Task Decisions

1. Map important entities and pages to meaningful navigable links and contextual anchor text.
2. Check orphan pages, crawl depth and broken destinations without adding repetitive keyword-stuffed links.

## Domain Focus

design internal linking, hubs, anchors, crawl paths, and equity flow without manipulative patterns.

## Official Sources

https://developers.google.com/search/docs | https://schema.org/docs/gs.html

## Guardrails

- Capture baselines before making recommendations that affect crawl, indexation, or traffic.
- Do not delete pages, publish redirects, change robots, or alter canonicals without explicit approval.
- Separate observed evidence from inferred search-impact estimates.
