# yandex direct account audit

## Task Decisions

1. Reconcile account identity, period, spend, VAT/currency, goals and conversion lag with Metrica/CRM evidence.
2. Separate confirmed defects from hypotheses and preserve valuable traffic; no automatic campaign optimization.

## Domain Focus

audit Yandex Direct account structure, spend, delivery, conversion evidence, and safe optimization backlog.
