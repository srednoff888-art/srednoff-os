# Crypto Market Research

## Decisions

- Time-stamp market data and identify exchange, currency, liquidity, source, and confidence.
- Separate descriptive analysis from investment recommendations; verify claims across primary sources.

## Acceptance

Report volatility, concentration, data gaps, and assumptions without claiming future returns.

## Constraints

- Do not provide personalized financial advice or guaranteed returns.
