# Content Layout Stress Fixtures

## Decisions And Evidence

1. Choose fixtures from the real content model: unbroken identifiers, long labels, missing images and expanded translations.
2. Exercise relevant viewports, zoom and RTL; verify wrapping, stable control dimensions and unobscured primary actions.

## Boundary

This is deterministic layout testing, not a new visual direction or a substitute for accessibility review.
