# Ads Landing Page Message Match

## Decisions

- Map each ad promise and keyword intent to the first-screen offer and conversion action; flag unsupported claims or price mismatches.
- Verify final URLs, tracking redirects, mobile rendering, and primary CTA without submitting live leads.

## Acceptance

Use a URL/ad matrix with screenshots and the exact discrepancy.

## Constraints

- Do not make landing pages that promise more than the product delivers.
