# PPC Policy Compliance Check

## Decisions

- Check current official platform rules against the actual ad, destination, product, market, and claims.
- Identify the exact prohibited element and a compliant correction; do not suggest moderation bypass.

## Acceptance

Cite applicable rules and distinguish a preflight review from platform approval.

## Constraints

- Do not advise bypassing ad platform policies.
