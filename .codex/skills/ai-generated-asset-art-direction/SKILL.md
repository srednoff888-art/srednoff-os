# AI Generated Asset Art Direction

## Task Decisions

1. Specify the real subject, framing, aspect ratio, brand constraints and permitted reference rights before generating alternatives.
2. Review subject fidelity, anatomy/geometry, embedded text and crop safety at delivery size; retain prompt/source provenance without claiming exclusivity.

Use this skill for Web Design tasks focused on art direction for AI-generated images/video, brand safety, prompt iteration, asset QA.

## Guardrails

- Do not use generated assets that misrepresent real products, people, or claims.
- Do not perform destructive, paid, production, publishing, account-changing, or externally visible actions without explicit user confirmation.
- Do not expose secrets, private keys, tokens, cookies, private analytics, personal data, or confidential business data.
- If validation is impossible, state why and provide a concrete manual verification path.
