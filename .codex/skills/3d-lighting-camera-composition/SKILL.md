# 3D Lighting Camera Composition
Use the asset's bounds and product geometry to frame the camera; test portrait and landscape layouts.
## Decisions
- Establish scene units, exposure, color space, tone mapping, and HDRI rights before adjusting lights.
- Prefer a small controlled lighting rig; budget shadows and environment-map resolution.
- Keep the subject inspectable and avoid clipping, excessive reflections, dark presentation, or decorative effects hiding product detail.
## Acceptance
Compare reference and rendered views, check model scale and materials, and capture mobile/desktop screenshots. Measure frame cost after changing shadows, maps, or post-processing.
