# Content Decay Pruning

## Task Decisions

1. Separate seasonality, demand changes, indexing defects and genuine content decay using comparable periods.
2. Preserve pages with conversions or useful links unless a justified merge/redirect plan exists; monitor the changed cohort separately.

Use this skill for SEO tasks focused on content decay analysis, pruning, merging, updating, redirecting, recovery prioritization.

## Guardrails

- Do not delete or redirect pages solely because recent traffic is low.
- Do not perform destructive, paid, production, publishing, account-changing, or externally visible actions without explicit user confirmation.
- Do not expose secrets, private keys, tokens, cookies, private analytics, personal data, or confidential business data.
- If validation is impossible, state why and provide a concrete manual verification path.
