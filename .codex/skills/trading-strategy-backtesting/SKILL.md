# Trading Strategy Backtesting

## Decisions

- Specify data provenance, timezone, universe, execution rules, fees, slippage, and split periods.
- Prevent lookahead/survivorship leakage; separate tuning from held-out evaluation.

## Acceptance

Report reproducible inputs, drawdown, turnover, costs, and sensitivity.

## Constraints

- Do not treat backtest performance as proof of future results.
