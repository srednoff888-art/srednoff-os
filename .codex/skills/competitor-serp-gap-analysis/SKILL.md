# Competitor Serp Gap Analysis

## Task Decisions

1. Compare pages serving the same query intent and market, recording location, device, date and SERP evidence.
2. Rank gaps by business relevance and evidence quality; do not treat word count or competitor traffic estimates as ranking causes.

Use this skill for SEO tasks focused on SERP competitors, content gaps, backlink/content angles, prioritization, opportunity mapping.
