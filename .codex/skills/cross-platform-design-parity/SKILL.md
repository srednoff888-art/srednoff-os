# Cross Platform Design Parity

## Decisions

- Map the same user goal to native platform navigation, controls, typography, and accessibility behavior.
- Keep business state consistent while respecting platform conventions rather than forcing pixel identity.

## Acceptance

Compare equivalent flows on each platform and record deliberate differences.

## Constraints

- Do not force identical UI where platform conventions require adaptation.
