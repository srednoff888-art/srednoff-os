# Scroll Driven 3D Storytelling

## Decisions

- Map content chapters and scroll progress to a bounded scene timeline with accessible text content.
- Preserve page scrolling and reduced motion; avoid loading all assets before useful content.

## Acceptance

Test touch scrolling, resize/back navigation, hidden-tab pausing, and mobile fallback.

## Constraints

- Do not make scroll effects that trap users or break keyboard navigation.
