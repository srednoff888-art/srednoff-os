# Motion Design System

## Decisions

- Define motion tokens, transition roles, easing, duration, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Keep component transitions interruptible and consistent across navigation and feedback states.

## Acceptance

Review representative flows under rapid input, reduced motion, and performance profiling.

## Constraints

- Do not add inconsistent one-off animation patterns.
