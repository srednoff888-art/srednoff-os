# PPC Budget Bid Optimizer
Confirm the platform, currency, reporting timezone, conversion/value definition, and permitted account scope.
## Decisions
- Separate incomplete days, reporting delay, conversion lag, and seasonality from actual deterioration.
- Evaluate pacing and marginal return, not only average ROAS. Avoid double-counting conversions across platforms.
- Draft changes with current state, rationale, bounded limits, expected measurement window, and rollback.
## Acceptance
Reconcile spend and conversion totals to source reports. For authorized execution, re-read changed objects and record exact scope. Recommendations alone do not establish business uplift.
