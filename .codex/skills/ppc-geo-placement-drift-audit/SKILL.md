# Ppc Geo Placement Drift Audit

## Decisions And Evidence

1. Compare platform changelog, current effective settings and observed delivery; separate defaults from explicit overrides.
2. Quantify out-of-scope spend/conversions with report completeness and lag, then draft the narrowest reversible change.

## Boundary

Do not add negatives, change geo settings or cut placements without approval and valuable-traffic counterexamples.
