# Pricing Onboarding UX

## Decisions

- Clarify price units, recurring charges, trial boundaries, entitlements, and the first useful action.
- Expose commitment and error recovery; keep billing facts consistent with the backend.

## Acceptance

Test plan selection, refusal/back paths, and account/entitlement states.

## Constraints

- Do not obscure costs, limits, or cancellation-relevant facts.
